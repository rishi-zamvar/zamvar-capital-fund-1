How we grow it into a real universe of movers

The engine is sound; it just needs a deeper pool so the "keep only the movers" cut actually bites. Three levers:

Measure moves on trial readouts + advisory-committee votes too — not only PDUFAs

Big biotech stock moves come from clinical-trial results and FDA advisory votes just as much as from approval dates. We already have that data. Counting those as "measurable events" multiplies the history per company — far more names reach ≥2 measurable moves, so the pool clears the target and the cut keeps only genuine movers. (Note: this widens what we consider a "catalyst move" beyond your original PDUFA-only spec — your call.)

Add a hard floor: a name must move ≥ ~10% to qualify

Independent of pool size, refuse to admit a name whose typical catalyst move is below a floor (say 10%). This alone drops the weak tail (BMRN, BCRX, VRDN…) tonight and guarantees the universe is only real movers — even before the pool grows.

Extend the lookback beyond 5 years

Going to ~8–10 years gives each company more past decisions to measure. Helps, but slower to add than the two above, and older reactions are less representative of today.

What's trustworthy right now

The top 6 (AQST, CRNX, URGN, CRSP, VNDA, INSM) are a real, high-conviction core — proven double-digit movers, measured from actual past price reactions, computed for free from data we already had (no API calls). The machinery — search, measure, rank, screen, write — ran clean and reconciles exactly with the stored universe. What's thin is the pool, and the two recommended levers fix that directly.

Recommendation: add the ≥10% floor now (kills the weak tail immediately) and widen measurable events to readouts + AdComs (grows the pool toward 80 real movers), then re-run — it's cheap and cached.
