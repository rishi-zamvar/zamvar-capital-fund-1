◆ Which catalyst sources actually work (free)

✅ CT.gov
Trial readouts — working, shown above. The government trial registry: authoritative, free, no key. Gives the watch-list of which names read out in which month. This is the primary biotech catalyst and our main tradeable supply.
✅ Nasdaq
Earnings dates — working, free. Date-precise. Pulled on the daemon's schedule (one call/day covers the market — too slow to pull inline here, so not in the table above). A secondary catalyst.
❌ PDUFA
FDA decision dates — no free authoritative source. FedRegister publishes AdCom meeting notices only (never PDUFA target dates); the old RTTNews scraper died (site went JS). This is the one real gap — it needs a hand-curated table (see below).

! The honest caveats (so this number isn't oversold)

Dates are mostly month-precision

8 of 9 are "expected October," not a specific day. CT.gov's primaryCompletionDate firms up as a trial nears completion; the exact readout day is confirmed by the company's press release / 8-K near the event. So this is the watch-list — the T-2 trading trigger fires once a date firms up.

Ingested ≠ tradeable (yet)

These flow into the store today, but our verify gate (DATA-03) requires 2 sources, and a CT.gov readout carries one authoritative source. The pending decision — allow a single authoritative source to verify — is what flips these from "ingested" to "tradeable." One-line policy call.

Next 30 days is thin; October is the cluster

Only RGNX reads out inside the next month (and month-precision at that). The real density is late October — 8 readouts, two firm on Oct 31 (SRPT, OLMA). A one-week-hold desk would be quiet now and busy into October.

⚙ How we build our own calendar

We already do — this page is it, generated from our free providers. The pipeline assembles the calendar on the daemon's schedule and keeps it warm:

CT.gov readouts + Nasdaq earnings + [curated PDUFA]  →  provenance-tagged Catalysts  →  2-source verify gate  →  tradeable calendar  →  T-2 trigger → the desk
  1. Readouts + earnings are automated + free — CT.gov (by curated lead-sponsor name) and Nasdaq, refreshed by the always-on daemon, cross-checked, cite-or-drop.
  2. PDUFA is the one manual piece — no free authoritative source exists, so it needs a small hand-curated table (data/pdufa_curated.csv): ticker, drug, PDUFA date, and its two confirming sources (FDA/company PR + a tracker). ~10 min per name, and only for names with a filing under review. Once curated, it joins the same calendar.
  3. The verify gate is the switch — decide whether a single authoritative source (CT.gov, FDA PR) verifies, or every date needs two. That decision turns this watch-list into a tradeable calendar.
