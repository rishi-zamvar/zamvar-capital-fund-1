The organizing idea how to read everything below

The desk is a hedge fund modeled in software. Analysts and desks propose; deterministic tools compute; the MD decides; you (the LP) are bothered only on a genuine blocker. Agents never do money arithmetic — every number comes from a tested tool. Nothing is claimed without a cited source. And a second desk vets every trade blind to the first desk's thesis.

Two speeds, one firm. Heavy Opus/Sonnet reasoning runs once per catalyst and once per week (the deciding). Cheap Haiku reasoning runs constantly by exception — the weekly company scan and the intra-week monitor only "wake up" when deterministic rules flag a real change. That keeps it both smart and cheap.

The system in layers foundation → workflows → decision → you

Everything rests on a verified-data + provenance foundation. The agentic workflows sit on top and only ever call the deterministic tools below them. The MD sits above all workflows; you sit above the MD.

A "workflow" is a group of LLM agents that reason together toward one output. Inside each, green chips are deterministic (a tested tool, no LLM); the rest are agent roles.

The weekly cadence conductor. Scans every name, shortlists, runs the desk, assembles the book toward ~1% within survival caps, and guarantees a never-empty book (defensive/cash-like on a weak week).

5 · MD / GP Reconciliation Loop & Router

desk/md.py → reconcileplanned · phase 6.7 (in build)

The decision hub. Every workflow proposes UP to the MD with a full decision packet. The MD reviews, and if it spots a gap re-triggers Workflow 1 or 2 with the missing info (never the answer) — a bounded loop (cap 3, oscillation → pass). Also the router for every flag. Escalates to you only on a genuine blocker.
