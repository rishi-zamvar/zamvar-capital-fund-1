Phase 7 — Verifier/Guardrails + Backtest Harness

● VERIFIED · 8/8 must-haves · 919 tests · the honesty milestone
2026-08-15 · BACK-01…05 + OPS-03 closed · goal: prove the corrected strategy honestly against history, and stand up the safety gates — before any capital.

✓ What's done

  • Two backtests, deliberately different in what you can trust — a Layer A mechanical replay (no AI at all) gives a trustworthy baseline: win rate, avg win/loss, Sharpe, Sortino, max drawdown, hedge-effectiveness. A Layer B guarded-agentic replay runs the real desk on history and is explicitly labeled "biased-optimistic" because an AI reading old cases can leak future knowledge — honesty over flattery.
  • The backtest measures the CORRECTED strategy — the weekly cadence, target-within-survival-cap sizing, no-zero-trade defensive weeks, the measured thesis engine — not some idealized version.
  • The fill model is pessimistic on purpose — a trade held through a binary event fills at the gapped open, never at the stop level (a stop can't save you across a gap). So the baseline can't flatter itself on exactly the risk that matters most in biotech.
  • No look-ahead — even on the free-data lag you flagged — a data-latency knob replays only what would actually have been known at decision time (delayed quotes, bi-monthly short interest). The review even caught two silent leaks (a catalyst "verified" after the fact, a direction read from empty fields) and both are now fixed with tests.
  • Survivorship-free — the event set includes delisted names, so the backtest isn't quietly cheating by only testing survivors.
  • Runtime safety gates, fail-closed — a pre-trade guardrail gate enforces catalyst-date freshness, cite-or-drop provenance, the diligence firewall, concentration caps, and "no scraped content can become an order" — any single check failing blocks the trade.
  • An honest go/no-go gate — sample-based, never calendar-based: too few historical trades = automatic no-go, no matter how good the numbers look. 919 tests, fully offline, zero new dependencies.

★ What's going well

Plan-check caught its sharpest bugs yet — the 7th straight money phase. Two silent look-ahead leaks that the written tests would have passed: a catalyst table with no time-guard (so history could "see" a catalyst verified later), and a direction estimate reading fields that are empty for catalyst trades (a hidden coin-flip). These are exactly the failure modes a backtest exists to prevent — caught at plan time, before a misleading number could ever be reported.
