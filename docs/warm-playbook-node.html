ZAMVAR CAPITAL · FUND 1 · NODE REDESIGN

What one event carries — now with the narrative

Redesigned so each past catalyst is a written CASE, not a row of enums. The structured fields route retrieval; the narrative body is what the model reasons from — and what we embed, so analogs match on the situation's story, not a label.

The core principle (how DoorDash, Sierra, Decagon, Letta, Zep all do it): a small structured envelope the retrieval layer reads deterministically, around a rich natural-language body the model actually reasons from. The hard facts (outcome, signed move) are code-verified and read_only — never LLM-trusted. The narrative gets embedded for semantic similarity. Facts route; stories reason.

A single node, fully written — Capricor (CAPR), 2025

Illustrative of what the Sonnet extractor writes per event (grounded in the real deramiocel CRL; exact driver values populated from the filings at build).

CATALYST NODE — CAPR · PDUFA · Duchenne cardiomyopathy · deramiocel
Envelope (facts, read-only)
outcome: CRLmove: −33%knowable_asof: 2025-04CMC: flaggedadcom: none
thesis
First cardiac cell therapy up for accelerated approval in DMD cardiomyopathy — a genuinely novel modality in an area of high unmet need but thin pivotal support.
causal_setup
Capricor sought approval largely on a single ~Phase-2 dataset (HOPE-2) plus open-label extension; a small, allogeneic cell-therapy program in a rare indication where the agency had signaled it wanted confirmatory evidence. The company had a partner (Nippon Shinyaku) and had been building commercial-scale manufacturing in parallel with review.
reference_class
Novel cell/gene therapies filed on a single small trial in rare disease: this reference class approves far below the ~85–90% base rate for drugs that reach BLA, and manufacturing/CMC is the single most common rejection cause (~29% of CRLs) — often on a drug the market assumed was clean on efficacy.
why_narrative ← the meat
The FDA issued a Complete Response Letter centered on chemistry-manufacturing-and-controls and the sufficiency of the single-study evidence base, not a new efficacy failure. Read in context, this fits a long regulatory pattern: the agency is structurally cautious on first-in-class cell therapies carrying scale-up risk, and a company racing manufacturing alongside review is exactly where CMC gaps surface. The −33% reaction reflects the market having priced a coin-flip-to-favorable outcome that the CMC/evidence bar overrode.
lesson_rule → becomes a playbook node
For single-trial cell/gene therapies with in-flight manufacturing, weight CMC-driven CRL risk far above the naive BLA base rate — and note that a CMC-only CRL is often resolvable (resubmit → approve), so the post-drop path can diverge sharply from an efficacy CRL. Watch the CRL's stated reason before fading or buying the dip.
second_order
A read-through to other single-trial rare-disease cell-therapy names on the calendar; potential financing pressure on CAPR (raise into a delayed timeline).
citations
CAPR 8-K / press release (CRL announcement) · CT.gov HOPE-2 · signed move T-1→T+2

Two nodes with the same label, opposite meaning

This is the whole reason for the narrative layer: Fortress / CUTX-101 got a CRL that was purely CMC → resubmitted → approved ~3 months later (the dip was a buy). A CRL where the FDA rejects the efficacy/trial design is often near-terminal. Same outcome = CRL, same enums — only the why_narrative and lesson_rule tell the model which future it's looking at.

How it's stored, embedded, retrieved

• Stored on the existing kb.py nodes (envelope in attrs_json; facts on the RESULTED_IN outcome; every claim CITES its source). Zero schema rebuild.
• Embedded: the composed narrative card (thesis + causal_setup + reference_class + why_narrative) is what goes into the vector index — so retrieval matches on the situation, not keywords.
• Retrieved: analog_query filters by type/indication + knowable_asof ≤ decision date, ranks by narrative similarity, and hands the model the top cases — each a full written precedent with its outcome, its why, and its lesson.
• Versioned bi-temporally (invalidate, don't overwrite) so a later revision never rewrites what was knowable then.
• Generalizations: recurring lesson_rules graduate into playbook nodes (e.g. "CMC-only CRL → resubmit-and-approve pattern").
