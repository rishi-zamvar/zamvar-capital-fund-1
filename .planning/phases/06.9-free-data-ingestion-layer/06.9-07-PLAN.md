<objective>
Wire the providers to the store — the INGEST-03 + INGEST-04 payoff. Two pieces in `ingest.py`:
1. **`assemble_raw(providers, ticker, week_end)`** — builds the `raw` dict `build_card` consumes (options from CBOE, short-interest from FINRA, filings from EDGAR, catalyst-derived fields), from the real providers. This is the ONE genuine injection seam; `build_card` is UNCHANGED. It INJECTS `catalyst_date` into `options_quote` from the ticker's next-catalyst `event_date` (the same date it derives for `days_to_catalyst`/`next_catalyst`) so the CBOE provider's 7-market-key quote becomes the COMPLETE 8-key quote `options_liquid` (desk/diligence.py, raising subscripts) needs — no KeyError, no crash into the pipeline. When CBOE returned None, `options_quote` stays None and build_card degrades. No `live=True/False` boolean threaded through the desk (RESEARCH Pattern 3 — the store IS the seam; the toggle is who populates it, resolved here at the entrypoint).
2. **The historical ingestion loop** over `data/seed_universe.csv` (~1-2yr): `get_weekly_bars` -> `upsert_bars(source, source_grade, agreeing_sources)`; catalysts (clinicaltrials + fmp.fetch_earnings + pdufa) -> `insert_catalyst` **THEN** `verify_catalyst` (the provider only inserts — the loop actually VERIFIES) with a POINT-IN-TIME `last_verified_at` stamp derived from the FR source's `publication_date` (NOT now — a naive now-stamp reintroduces the Phase-7 look-ahead leak); cards -> `assemble_raw` + `build_card` + `write_card`. Idempotent by construction (UPSERT), point-in-time + survivorship-aware.

Purpose: real store rows the backtest/scanner read via the SAME signatures, with a REAL look-ahead-free verified event set. The offline suite proves the wiring; the LIVE run is Plan 08.
Output: `ingest.py` + `test_ingest_seam.py` + `test_ingest_historical.py`.
