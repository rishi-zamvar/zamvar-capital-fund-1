# Phase 06.9 Plan 07: ingest.py — assemble_raw Seam + Historical Ingestion Loop Summary

The provider↔store seam that ties the whole free-data layer together: `assemble_raw` builds the exact `raw` dict `build_card` consumes (injecting `catalyst_date` so the CBOE quote is the complete 8-key `options_liquid` shape), and `ingest_history` loops the seed universe funneling prices/catalysts/cards through the existing idempotent UPSERT paths with a point-in-time, look-ahead-free verified event set.

## What was built

`src/catalyst_trader/ingest.py` — two pieces, zero new dependencies, every write through the shipped store paths:

1. **`assemble_raw(providers, ticker, week_end) -> dict`** (INGEST-03): calls the datum providers (`options`/`finra`/`edgar`/optional `financials`) + derives the catalyst-window fields (`next_catalyst`, `days_to_catalyst`, `catalysts_4w`) from the ticker's upcoming catalysts, assembling exactly the keys `build_card` reads. **The injection seam:** if the CBOE provider returned a 7-market-key `options_quote`, it stamps `options_quote["catalyst_date"] = next_catalyst` so `options_liquid` (raising subscripts, `desk/diligence.py`) sees the complete 8-key dict. A `None` quote — or no upcoming catalyst to gate against — leaves `options_quote=None` and `build_card` degrades to the in-house path. Never a partial dict.

2. **`ingest_history(conn, *, start, end, providers, universe_csv=...)`** (INGEST-04): reads the seed CSV (skips `#` lines), then over `[start, end]`:
   - **prices** → `_upsert_price_bars` groups `get_weekly_bars` output by `(source, source_grade, agreeing_sources)` and funnels each group through `store.upsert_bars(delisted=…, source_grade=…, agreeing_sources=…)` — survivorship-aware, provenance-tagged.
   - **catalysts** (clinicaltrials + fmp earnings + pdufa) → `insert_catalyst` **THEN** `verify_catalyst`; on `Status.verified`, `_pit_stamp` reads the authoritative (primary-preferred) agreeing source's in-memory `published_date` and stamps `last_verified_at` (point-in-time). A curated `company_map` (ticker → `[company_name]`) from the seed feeds FedRegister attribution.
   - **cards** → `assemble_raw` + `build_card` + `write_card` per `(ticker, week_end)` with bars.
