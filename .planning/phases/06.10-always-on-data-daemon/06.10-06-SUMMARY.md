# Phase 06.10 Plan 06: Always-on Data Daemon Summary

The thin always-on daemon over the now-complete provider set: a single `build_providers` registry
(both the daemon and the shakedown wire through it), a stateless idempotent `run_cycle()`, self-heal
(gap-detect + backfill + dead-source failover), the `.monitor/data_health.json` writer with an honest
per-cycle `divergences_this_cycle`, a monitor Data-health tab + alerts, and a launchd plist that
supervises one `--once` cycle on a 6h cadence with KeepAlive-on-failure crash-recovery. NO long-lived
loop, NO queue, $0 — launchd is the supervisor, store idempotency is the resumability. Reuses the
entire 6.9 ingest/store/cascade machinery unchanged.

## What shipped

**Task 1 — `providers/registry.py` (single registry + CADENCE).** `build_providers(settings, end=)`
lifts what was `shakedown._live_providers`'s duplicated dict into one place and wires the Wave-1/2
providers: `edgar.fetch`→`edgar`, `edgar.fundamentals`→`financials`, `fred.fetch_macro`→`macro`
(degrades to `None` without a FRED key), `finra.fetch`→`finra`, and
`fetch_earnings_with_fallback(fmp.fetch_earnings, nasdaq_earnings.fetch)`→`earnings`. The per-ticker
`_options`/`_finra`/`_edgar`/`_financials` closures KEEP their `@functools.cache` (verified by a test:
three calls → one underlying hit). `CADENCE` carries a seconds interval per slot. `shakedown._live_providers`
now just returns `registry.build_providers(settings, end=end)` — the duplicate dict and
`_recent_si_settlement` are gone (moved to `registry.recent_si_settlement`).

**Task 2 — `pipeline.py` + `scripts/daemon.py` + launchd plist.**
- `detect_gaps` — expected (universe × Friday) name-weeks vs present `price_bar`; stale price sources
  (freshest `retrieved_at` older than 2× cadence via `prices.is_stale`); upcoming-catalyst names with no card.
- `backfill` — re-feeds gaps through the SAME `ingest.ingest_history` path over a widened start
  (idempotent upsert, no guard bypass); a no-gap cycle is a no-op.
- `write_data_health` — `{updated, sources:{name:{last_success,consec_fail,degraded}}, coverage_pct,
  stale_sources, gaps, divergences_this_cycle, degraded}`; `coverage_pct` from the persisted
  `agreeing_sources` column; `divergences_this_cycle` passed in from the log-tap.
- `daemon.run_cycle` — one pass: ingest → detect_gaps → backfill → post-heal detect_gaps → source
  states (consec_fail carried forward across cycles) → `write_data_health`. A `_DivergenceTap`
  logging.Handler counts the cycle's cross-check divergences. `scripts/daemon.py --once` builds
  conn+providers and calls it once, then exits.
- launchd plist: `StartInterval 21600` (6h), `KeepAlive/SuccessfulExit=false` (restart only on crash),
  `RunAtLoad`, std out/err to `.monitor/`.

**Task 3 — monitor Data-health tab + alerts.** `data_health()` folds the JSON into `sample()` and
computes an `alerts` list (degraded source / coverage < 80% / divergence spike ≥ 5). A best-effort,
de-duped ZeeChat ping (`Popen`, non-blocking, swallow-all) fires on new alerts and can never block or
crash the monitor. The PAGE gains a Data-health tab rendering per-source last_success/consec_fail/degraded,
coverage %, gap counts, stale list, and `divergences_this_cycle`.
