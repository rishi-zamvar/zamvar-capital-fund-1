<objective>
Build the fact-check verifier: before a `DebateVerdict` is finalized, resolve every
`evidence_ref` and enforce source-quote-or-drop. The structural half — ref resolves and the
cited quote is a substring of the source snippet — is deterministic Python (`desk.evidence`).
The semantic half — does the quote actually support the claim — is an LLM call, used ONLY for
load-bearing claims. Dropping a load-bearing claim blocks finalize.

Purpose: CALL-04 — no claim survives into the verdict without a resolvable, supporting source.
This is the reasoning-layer mirror of the KB's provenance invariant.
Output: `desk/verifier.py`.
</objective>
