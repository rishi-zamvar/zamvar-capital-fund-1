<objective>
Build `desk/builder.py`: DebateVerdict → TradeProposal. This is the single-leg estimated-move
construction engine (the no-hedge case is the DEFAULT, and it is engineered, not "size tiny and pray").
It selects a candidate inverse peer (handed to Risk for validation), sets entry, estimates the
catalyst's +/- payoff distribution (param-driven, cited), VETS that estimate via `enforce_estimate`,
decides hold-through-vs-exit-before by expected-log-growth + a survivable tradeable size, and sets
advisory-only SL/TP. Every threshold is a loaded param; the estimated down-move (not a blanket -70%)
sizes the leg.

Purpose: PORT-02 (trade construction) + HEDGE-01/04 (candidate peer + honest fallback shape).
Output: `desk/builder.py` + TDD suite + the trade_builder_proposal fixture.
</objective>
