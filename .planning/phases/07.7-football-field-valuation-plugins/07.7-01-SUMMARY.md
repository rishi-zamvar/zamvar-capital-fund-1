# Phase 07.7 Plan 01: Football-Field Valuation Plugins Summary

**Two net-new canonical quant plugins — the `expected_move_field` convergence aggregator (confidence-weighted trimmed median + CV dispersion) and the `analyst_pt_move` street-price-target move method — TDD-covered, frozen-ToolResult, and registered in the `catalyst_trader.quant` import surface.**

## Performance

- **Duration:** ~12 min
- **Tasks:** 2/2 (TDD RED → GREEN)
- **Files created:** 2 (field.py, test_field.py)
- **Files modified:** 3 (valuation.py, __init__.py, test_valuation.py)

## Accomplishments
- `expected_move_field` aggregator: sorts contributions by magnitude, applies symmetric fractional-weight trim, takes the cumulative-weight median (straddle → average), and emits `{contributions, point_estimate, low, high, dispersion, agreement, n_methods, method_labels, refs}`. Raises below `min_methods`, on negative/non-finite magnitude, on non-positive/non-finite weight, and on a missing `params["field"]` key.
- `analyst_pt_move`: `median(|pt-price|/price)` with IQR/median dispersion (inclusive quartiles); guards `current_price<=0`, empty list, and any non-finite target/price.
- Both registered in `quant/__init__.py` (imports + `__all__`, new `# field` group).

## Deviations from Plan
None — plan executed as written. The plan/RESEARCH flagged (Open Q 3) that a symmetric trim rarely moves a MEDIAN; the trim test accordingly asserts on the observable effect (extreme contributions dropped from the post-trim low/high and the survivor dispersion collapsing) rather than asserting the point estimate changed. This matches the plan's stated behavior ("drops the most extreme contributions symmetrically before the median") and is not a scope change.
