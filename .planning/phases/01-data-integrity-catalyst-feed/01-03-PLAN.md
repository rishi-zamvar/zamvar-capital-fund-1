<objective>
Build the two API providers that auto-populate the calendar (DATA-01): the ClinicalTrials.gov v2 client for trial readouts and the FMP client for earnings. Each returns typed records with provenance already attached — a date physically cannot leave a provider without a named source. Parsing is tested entirely against the Plan-01 fixtures (offline, deterministic); no live API calls in the test suite.

Purpose: These are two of the three feeds behind the rolling calendar. They must honor the two verified CT.gov gotchas: partial dates (route through the Plan-02 classifier) and sponsor fuzziness (curated lead-sponsor filter).
Output: `providers/base.py`, `providers/clinicaltrials.py`, `providers/fmp.py`, a starter ticker->sponsor map, and their fixture-driven tests.
</objective>
