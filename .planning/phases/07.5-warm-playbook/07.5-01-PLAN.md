<objective>
Define the warm-playbook RECORD CONTRACT and the narrative-card composer as a pure, DB-free,
LLM-free module on the EXISTING kb.py scaffold (ZERO migration — everything lands in attrs_json).
This is the contract every later task in the phase builds against: the extractor (03) emits a
CatalystRecord, the KB writer (04) maps it to catalyst/outcome node attrs + the embedded card, and
generalization (05) reads the narrative fields back.

Two layers per catalyst (LOCKED DECISIONS, research D+E):
- STRUCTURED ENVELOPE (deterministic, filters retrieval, read-only facts): ticker, type, indication,
  modality, knowable_asof (the leak gate), event_date, outcome_label, SIGNED move, confidence,
  move_consistency_flag, drivers{~18 taxonomy fields}.
- NARRATIVE BODY (the reasoning substrate that gets embedded): before {thesis, causal_setup,
  reference_class, variant_why, what_to_watch, what_would_change_our_mind}; after {what_happened,
  why_narrative, surprise_vs_prior, which_disconfirmer_fired, lesson_rule, second_order, process_grade}.

Purpose: a single typed contract so a different Claude can extract/write/read records without
re-deriving the shape. Cite-or-drop is honored downstream by kb.insert_claim; here the citations
list is a required, non-empty field on the contract.
Output: src/catalyst_trader/playbook/schema.py + a TDD suite.
</objective>
