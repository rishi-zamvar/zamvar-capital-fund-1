# Phase 06.7 Plan 01: MD/GP Reconciliation Contracts Summary

**Four pydantic contracts making the phase's money-critical firewalls type facts: you cannot hand a conclusion down a re-trigger (RetriggerFlag) or up a weekly flag (WeeklyFlag), cannot escalate without completed staff work (MDDecision), and every unactioned flag has a home in the round log (ReconciliationRound.logged_flags).**

- **Duration:** 8 min
- **Completed:** 2026-08-15
- **Tasks:** 2

## Accomplishments
- `RetriggerFlag` + `WeeklyFlag`: gap-not-answer firewalls in the type system — `extra="forbid"` + the absence of any answer-shaped field (direction/confidence/size/verdict) makes handing the debate/builder a conclusion a `ValidationError` at construction; `WeeklyFlag` additionally has NO `target`, so the weekly refresh cannot pre-assign the route (the MD does).
- `MDDecision` (standalone) + `ReconciliationRound`: the MD's sole output record with escalation gated by completed-staff-work (`blocker_class` + `recommended_resolution` required iff `escalate`, forbidden otherwise), plus an immutable per-round audit trail that logs both actioned re-triggers (`md_flags`) and unactioned incoming flags (`logged_flags`).
- 39 new boundary-rejection tests (forbids-answer, forbids-target, escalation-shape, empty-field, bad-literal, not-a-StrategyDossier-field regression guard); full suite 731 → 770, no regression.
