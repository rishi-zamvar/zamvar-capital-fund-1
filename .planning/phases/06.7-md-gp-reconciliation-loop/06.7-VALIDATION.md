- [ ] **Bounded loop (RECON-02/03/04):** cap=3 (range); convergence = deterministic `and` over contract fields + one MD boolean; oscillation (repeated evidence_ref) → forced PASS; "MD can always PASS"; per-round immutable `round_log`. - [ ] **No-regression:** `extra_context=None` leaves debate/builder byte-for-byte (prompt-equality tests); all RECON-01..07 covered; no live API.
