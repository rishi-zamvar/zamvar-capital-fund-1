# Phase 07.6 Plan 04: Leak-Property / Temporal Non-Interference Suite Summary

An adversarial test suite that ATTEMPTS to read post-cutoff data through the gateway and ASSERTS it is denied. It sweeps cutoffs across a known bitemporal timeline and, at every step, asserts the served set equals exactly the facts knowable by that cutoff (property-shaped, not a single case). Also proves: a revised catalyst returns the as-of-then version, an unstamped table is denied by default, and a derived stat over the book uses only pre-cutoff rows.

## What was built

- **tests/leak_fixtures.py**: `seed_leak_timeline(conn)` — bars (W1<W2<W3), a card at W2, a scan_flag recorded at R2, a universe cohort recorded at RU, an amended catalyst (D1@T1 → D2@T2), a catalyst_move at KM, and a deliberately-unstamped `playbook` table. Returns a `LeakTimeline` of the known dates.
- **tests/test_leak_property.py**: cutoff-sweep non-interference (per accessor), unstamped-denied, live-sees-all, as-of-then revision replay, no-whole-table-stat-leak, lag-closes-borderline.

## Verification

- `tests/test_leak_property.py` (11) green; every assertion routes through `serving.asof`.
- Full suite: **1238 passed** (1227 + 11), zero regressions.

## Invariants honored

- Post-cutoff facts provably denied through every accessor across a swept timeline. ✅

- Amended catalyst returns as-of-then version (append-never-overwrite replay). ✅
- Ingestion lag is part of the non-interference guarantee (borderline leak closed). ✅
