<objective>
Lay the bitemporal foundation: migration 011 adds the transaction-time stamps the audit found MISSING (`scan_flag.recorded_at`, `universe_snapshot.recorded_at`) and an append-only `catalyst_version` history table so amended catalyst dates are RETAINED as versions instead of overwritten. Change `insert_catalyst` to append a version row when (and only when) `event_date` changes, and add a `catalyst_version_asof` reader that resolves the event_date knowable at a cutoff. This is the "append-never-overwrite" half of decision A + the schema half of decision C.

Purpose: without retained versions, a pre-amendment replay is impossible (the current UPDATE-in-place destroys the old date). Foundation for the gateway (Plan 02) and the leak tests (Plan 04).
Output: migration 011 + backfill, `catalyst_version` history, append-on-amendment write, `catalyst_version_asof` reader, exhaustive bitemporal tests. NO gateway yet, NO consumer wiring yet.
