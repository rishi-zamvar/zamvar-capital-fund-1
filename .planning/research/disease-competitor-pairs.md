## 0. Rules of evidence (carried from prior studies)

- Same-disease is NOT sufficient (PHVS/BCRX moved together — anti-hedge).
- Same-mechanism rivals are read-through siblings, NOT hedges (ALNY/IONS, psychedelics herd) — excluded, but documented where tempting.
- Displacement flowing to a $50B+ mega-cap is unusable (SMMT→MRK, SION→VRTX) — flagged, not counted.
- n=1–3 event prints are ANECDOTES, never "significant." Daily-stat threshold for credibility ≈ |t|≥3 with mechanism + both-direction consistency (multiple-testing reality: ~5% of tests hit |t|≥2 by chance).
- Method reused: yfinance daily adjusted closes ~2yr; two-factor residuals (OLS on SPY+XBI); big-move-day co-movement (|resid_A|≥5%, response = sign(resid_A)×resid_B, mean/t/n); catalyst-day overlays where dates are verifiable.
- yfinance = research-grade only; never fabricate — UNVERIFIED marked as such.

- UTHR~LQDA resid corr −0.057 (n=500) — no standing inverse; big-move stats insignificant both ways (t −0.79…−0.91 at 5%).
- **Inverse prints exist:** 2026-02-25 UTHR +13.4% resid / LQDA −10.8% / INSM −5.6%, and the mirror next day 2026-02-26 UTHR −5.7% / LQDA +2.8% (event attribution UNVERIFIED — consistent with UTHR Q4'25 earnings week repricing the share war; verify before use). 2025-05-14 LQDA +17.3% / UTHR −1.9% (approval-anticipation window; attribution UNVERIFIED).

Usable hedges: long-LQDA against UTHR ralinepag/TETON regulatory disappointment (share war re-rates toward LQDA), long-UTHR against LQDA-specific commercial stumbles (2026-08-12 LQDA −11.5% / UTHR −1.2% shows this only partially pays).
