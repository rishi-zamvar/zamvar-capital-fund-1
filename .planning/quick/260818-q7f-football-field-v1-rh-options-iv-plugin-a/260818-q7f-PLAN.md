<objective>
Wire the trade builder to produce a real multi-method expected-move football field instead of
the analog-only estimate, plus an abstaining Robinhood-sourced options-IV data adapter and a
live-assumption injectable seam.

Purpose: The football-field convergence is the magnitude the whole desk anchors on. Today
`builder.build` estimates the move from analogs alone (`catalyst_move_estimate(..., implied_move=None)`).
This assembles analog + rNPV + analyst-target + sum-of-parts + options-IV into `expected_move_field`
and feeds the convergence back through the SAME sizing seam, and adds the builder-vs-DD discrepancy
loopback the MD reconciler needs. This closes FF-05 (football-field magnitude flows into builder.build;
DD re-derives dd_expected_move; cross_check compares; reconcile routes the discrepancy).

Output: an options-IV adapter, a 5-method field in the builder behind two injectable provider
seams, an independent DD expected-move number, and the cross_check discrepancy flag.

CRITICAL — this is ASSEMBLY, not estimator-building. The hard parts already exist and MUST be
reused verbatim (do NOT duplicate):
  - quant.field.expected_move_field(methods, params)  — the aggregator (trimmed weighted median)
  - quant.options.implied_move(underlying_price, call_mid, put_mid, iv, days_to_event)
  - quant.valuation.rnpv / analyst_pt_move / pipeline_value
  - quant.events.catalyst_move_estimate(..., implied_move=<prior>)  — ALREADY takes a magnitude prior
  - desk.md.cross_check / reconcile
  - kb.load_params(conn, ctype)['field'] — weights + min_methods + trim_frac + dispersion_wide + move_discrepancy_tol
No new magic numbers: every threshold/weight comes from load_params()['field'].
</objective>
