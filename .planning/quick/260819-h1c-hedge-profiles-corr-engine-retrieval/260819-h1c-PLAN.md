<objective>
Wave 1 of the hedge-profiles milestone (design spec sections 1a-1d): give the desk explicit,
always-available correlation/hedge facts per name, pulled out-of-band with retrieval but never
scored into the KNN vote.

Purpose: the strategy's names are ~90% idiosyncratic (peer pairs corr ~0.11 median; XBI explains
~10% of the median name's weekly variance). The "long target + correlated peer long" hedge premise
is largely unsupported. An agent must SEE, per name, whether a hedge is real (explicit verdict +
systematic/idiosyncratic split + a suggested_hedge string) rather than assuming 0.7-corr = hedged.

Output: a DB-aware correlation engine, KB storage/reader for company-node hedge profiles, an
out-of-band wiring into TradeProposal, and a populate/recompute CLI.

Scope guard: WAVE 1 ONLY. Do NOT build the portfolio-builder what-if engine (Wave 2, sections
2a-2b) — no desk/portfolio_builder.py, no desk/tools.py changes.
